# Project Charter: CampusHelp

| Component | Information |
|---|---|
| **Project title** | CampusHelp: University Student Service Request Portal |
| **Business problem** | Student service requests (IT support, classroom maintenance, library assistance, administrative services) arrive through emails and paper forms, so they are difficult to track. |
| **Project objective** | Plan and demonstrate a centralized, web-based request management portal. |
| **Project sponsor** | University Administration (simulated) |
| **Project manager** | Student A (defaultXcs) |
| **Stakeholders** | Students, IT support, administrators, maintenance staff |
| **Project duration** | 10 working days (17 Nov 2026 to 30 Nov 2026) |
| **Project budget** | Estimated using team hours: 45 h x 50,000 UZS per person-hour = 2,250,000 UZS |
| **In scope** | Request submission, request tracking, request assignment, status updates and dashboard mockups/prototype |
| **Out of scope** | Real authentication, production server, live SMS/email services |
| **Success criteria** | Four milestones completed and prototype verified (see measurable criteria below) |
| **Constraints** | Three team members, limited time (10 working days), no external service charges |

## Measurable success criteria

1. All 4 milestones (M1 to M4) are completed by their due dates: 18 Nov, 20 Nov, 25 Nov and 30 Nov 2026.
2. All 6 proposed system features (request submission, categories and priority, tracking by request ID, administrator assignment, status updates, dashboard) are represented in the requirements or interface designs.
3. At least 6 test cases are executed on the prototype and every failed test is documented with a follow-up action.
4. All 12 mandatory issues (T01 to T12) are closed with evidence (a document, prototype or Pull Request link).
5. Total actual effort stays within 10% of the 45 h estimate.

## Assumptions

- The prototype is a clickable or documented screen flow. A production application is not required.
- Each member works about 15 hours during the project.
- The sponsor is simulated by the lecturer.
