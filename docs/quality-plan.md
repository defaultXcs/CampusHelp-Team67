# Quality Plan

Related issue: #6 (task T06)

## Purpose

This plan explains how each deliverable is checked before it is accepted.

## Quality checks

| Deliverable | How it is checked | Who checks | Accepted when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Documents (charter, WBS, schedule, risks) | Reviewer reads the file in the pull request and compares it with the issue's acceptance criteria | A team member who is not the author | All required parts are present and the reviewer approves |
| Wireframes | Compared with the requirements (FR1 to FR7) | Another team member | Every required screen is shown |
| Prototypes | Opened in a browser and tested with the test cases | Student C, reviewed by Student B | Test cases pass and simulated parts are marked |
| Change request | Scope check, impact and decision are all written | Another team member | Decision is recorded with a reason |
| Final report | Checked against the success criteria and the board | Another team member | Results, hours and lessons learned are included |

## Rules for every task

- One branch per task, no direct commits to main.
- Every pull request is reviewed and approved by someone other than the author.
- The pull request says "Closes #N" and the issue links to its evidence.
- Actual hours are entered on the board after the merge.
- Simulated functions are clearly marked.
